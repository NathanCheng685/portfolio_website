# Nathan, illustrated and in pixels

## Illustrated (default)

Drawn by `scripts/build_illustration.py` from Nathan's October 2026 photos (white
T-shirt tucked into high-waisted black wide-leg trousers, black leather shoes) with
Korean-style bangs in a 4:6 side part, as Nathan asked: the back hugs the skull, a
sideburn runs to mid-ear with a lock behind the ear, and strands comb backward at the
back, fall straight down in the middle and sweep forward at the front. Slim through
the waist and hips. Faceless side view,
angular shapes, hard two-tone shading and a light grain, after a painted running
illustration he liked (style reference only; nothing traced). Poses come from joint
angles; the run cycle has 8 frames with a deep lean, high knee drive and a big arm
swing. The photos stay in his Downloads folder and are not copied into the repo.

## Pixel (fallback)

Pixel art in the style of the Codex preview, which Nathan preferred. The base parts
(head, shirt, legs, wave, phone, cooking) are the Codex artwork itself, rasterized from
its 64 × 96 path data by `scripts/build_sprite.py`. Poses it lacked are painted on the
same grid and palette: relaxed arms, blink, a head turned toward the direction of
travel (walking reuses the Codex stepping legs), magnifier, thinking, thumbs up.
Cells are 64 × 88 (rows 5 to 92). The site shows it at 1.5×, as Codex did.

## Reference

Only the tall young man in Nathan's `IMG_8197.HEIC`: center-parted black hair,
white T-shirt, loose black wide-leg pants, black shoes. The woman in the photo
(his aunt) and the background are excluded. No photo is copied into this repository
or served by the site. The sprite is a stylized likeness.

## Asset

`nathan-sprite.svg` is generated by `scripts/build_sprite.py`; edit the grids and
part functions there, then run `python3 scripts/build_sprite.py`. Frame order must
match `F` in `sprite.js`.

| Frames | Pose | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| stand, blink | Front, idle | Everywhere; blinks every few seconds |
| wave-a, wave-b | Greeting | Hello, Say hi |
| walk-0 to walk-3 | Stepping with the head turned | Scrolling between cards (mirrored when going back) |
| phone-a, phone-b | Looking at a phone | SmartCited |
| grip, grip-step-a, grip-step-b | Holding the age cursor, stepping | fMRI |
| pan-a, pan-b | Holding and tossing a pan | CookTree |
| look-a, look-b | Magnifier | Neural nets |
| think | Hand to chin | LLMs |
| thumbs | Thumbs up | Reactions |

Playback is stop-motion: whole frames on a timer, no tweening. Walk frames advance
with scrolled distance, so the stride matches the scroll. Reduced motion shows only
resting poses.
