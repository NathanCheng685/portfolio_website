# Interactive portfolio, English edition

The main portfolio since 2026-10-20 (the previous page is kept at `sites/classic/`,
live at `/classic/`). No build step, no dependencies, no analytics, no external calls.

## The idea

One question, many methods. The first card introduces Nathan; scrolling down then
walks a horizontal strip of project cards, Rauno-style: the focused card is large,
the camera pulls back between cards so neighbors show, and the cards tilt a little
while moving. Nathan stands on the ground line under the cards. Scrolling is him running
(an 8-frame run cycle, driven by distance), and each scene gives him its own pose:
waving, holding the age cursor, a magnifier, thinking, a phone, a pan. He is drawn
twice: an angular, faceless illustration (default, from his photos) and a pixel
version (backup, only with `?art=pixel`; there is no toggle on the page).

Scenes, in order of the methods he used:

| Scene | What a visitor does | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hello | Push the pixel question mark around; click to scatter it | Waves |
| fMRI (PNAS) | Drag Nathan along the age axis; the paper's per-window results (SI Table S8) are revealed, with both clusters' values for the current window and the regions named | Holds the cursor, walks with it |
| Cog models (SBI) | Turn four dials (α, β, φ, κ) until your simulated learner follows a real test learner's curve, then reveal the truth next to the network's estimate | Magnifier; thumbs up on a match |
| LLMs (thesis) | Take one of four real gambles, then see how people and two models chose as their reasoning grows, with a real reasoning quote; the left column holds the 100-gamble pilot result | Thinks; thumbs up after a pick |
| SmartCited | Tap citations, fix the café's own page, watch the AI answer change | Phone |
| CookTree | Tap pantry items in the ingredient tree, then cook | Tosses the pan |
| Say hi | Contact, links, four smaller projects | Waves goodbye |

Each card takes about 0.72 of a screen of scrolling, with a still zone around it.
When the visitor stops between two cards, the page glides on in the direction they
were going if they moved deliberately (past `COMMIT`), otherwise back. The glide is
its own eased animation (500 to 900 ms, ease-in-out sine) and yields to any input. Page Down, Space and the arrow keys
move one card. Cards keep a fixed landscape ratio (1.75 : 1) and the card plus
its ground line sit centered in the window, so narrow windows never squash them.

Screens narrower than 900 px or shorter than 620 px get a plain vertical stack,
with each card's own character in its caption. The OS reduced-motion setting removes
the zoom, tilt and frame animation.

## Preview

```sh
python3 scripts/serve.py
```

Then open <http://localhost:4184/>. The server sends `no-store`, so edits show on
reload. In the Claude desktop app the `portfolio-interactive` preview runs the same
script. Module scripts and the data JSON need HTTP; `file:` URLs do not work.

## Structure

- `index.html`: all seven cards, captions and the ground line.
- `site.css`: layout for track mode (`html.is-track`) and the stacked fallback.
- `site.js`: scroll to position mapping (with a still zone around each card),
  zoom and tilt, scrubber, walking, scene enter and leave, keyboard and hash links.
- `sprite.js`: frame map and the `Actor` class (stop-motion playback, blinking).
- `scenes/*.js`: one module per card.
- `models.js`: the seeded RLF learning simulator (the project's update rules).
- `data/asd-trajectories.json`, `data/sbi-learners.json`, `data/llm-gambles.json`: the
  real numbers behind the three research cards (provenance in `SOURCES.md`).
- `assets/nathan-illustrated.svg`: 23-frame illustrated atlas (160 × 210 cells), generated by
  `scripts/build_illustration.py` from joint angles per pose.
- `assets/nathan-sprite.svg`: 19-frame pixel atlas (64 × 88 cells, Codex style), generated by
  `scripts/build_sprite.py`.
- `scripts/export_gambles.py`: regenerates `data/llm-gambles.json` from the thesis repo
  (`python3 scripts/export_gambles.py "<path to cot-human-likeness>"`).

The previous Codex version (sequential gallery, lab page, earlier sprite) is kept
whole at `90 归档Archives/portfolio-interactive-codex-2026-10-19/`.
